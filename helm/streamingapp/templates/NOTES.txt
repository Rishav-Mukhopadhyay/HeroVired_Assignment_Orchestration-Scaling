StreamingApp has been deployed!

1. Get the public address (takes 2-3 minutes to appear under EXTERNAL-IP):
     kubectl get svc frontend

2. Tell the backends their public address (needed for login/CORS):
     helm upgrade streamingapp ./helm/streamingapp --reuse-values --set publicUrl=http://<EXTERNAL-IP>

3. Watch everything start:
     kubectl get pods -w
